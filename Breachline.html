Your first breach

  1. Select A, B or C. Bright tiles show reachable destinations (up to 3 steps). Click your destination. Click a closed door to approach it by up to 2 steps and open it.
  2. Plan one action for each unit: move, fire, approach and open a door, or hold. Units without orders hold position.
  3. Execute plans. The bot also plans in secret. Units approach and open planned doors, then both sides fire simultaneously.
  4. Occupy the terminal. Score a point only when no living enemy occupies the gold zone. Four points or all enemies eliminated: victory.

Firing requires visibility and a Manhattan distance of at most 7 tiles. Moving targets can leave the line of fire. Blocked or contested tiles stop movement. Gold edges mark low cover: it protects the unit on that tile from frontal shots (1 damage instead of 2), but not flanking shots. Walls block vision and shots. Vision cone: 120 degrees, range 7. Movement turns the unit in its travel direction. Guard faces the selected tile and fires once on first contact, including during enemy movement. A reaction shot can prevent a planned shot. Question marks show last known positions, not live tracking.

After 16 rounds, the higher score wins; equal scores are a draw. Training replays show both sides. Select a clip round or use automatic highlight selection. Video export takes about 10 seconds in a visible browser tab.
